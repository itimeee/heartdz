About This Dataset

  • The Cleveland Heart Disease dataset contains data from 303 patients
  • 14 clinical features including age, sex, chest pain type, blood pressure, cholesterol, and more
  • Target variable indicates presence (1) or absence (0) of heart disease
  • This data is used to predict the likelihood of heart disease based on various health metrics
